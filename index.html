❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What is an IP address? ▼
An IP (Internet Protocol) address is a unique numerical label assigned to every device connected to a computer network. It serves two main purposes: identifying the host or network interface, and providing the location of the device in the network. There are two versions: IPv4 (e.g., 192.168.1.1) and IPv6.
Can someone find my location from my IP? ▼
Yes, partially. Your IP reveals your approximate city, ISP, and country — but NOT your exact home address. Websites you visit can see your IP. Using a VPN hides your real IP by routing traffic through another server.
What's the difference between IPv4 and IPv6? ▼
IPv4 uses 32-bit addresses (e.g., 203.0.113.5) with ~4.3 billion possible addresses. IPv6 uses 128-bit addresses (e.g., 2001:db8::1) with virtually unlimited addresses, created to solve IPv4 exhaustion. Most modern devices support both.
How do I hide or change my IP address? ▼
You can hide your IP using: (1) A VPN — most popular method, encrypts all traffic. (2) Tor Browser — routes through multiple relays. (3) Proxy Server — masks IP for specific applications. For most users, a trusted VPN is the best option.
What is my ISP and why does it matter? ▼
Your ISP (Internet Service Provider) is the company providing your internet connection. They can see your browsing activity (unless you use HTTPS + VPN), and your IP address is assigned by them. Your ISP is required to respond to legal requests about your IP activity.
Is this tool safe? Does it store my IP? ▼
This tool uses third-party APIs (ipapi.co) to fetch your IP info. We do not store or log your IP address on our end. The IP data is fetched real-time and displayed only to you. Always check the privacy policies of any IP tool you use.
